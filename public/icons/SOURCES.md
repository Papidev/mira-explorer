# Source logos

These logos identify the projects used as data sources. Their trademarks remain
with their respective owners; their display does not imply endorsement.

- `wikipedia-w.svg`: existing [Wikipedia W](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wikipedia%27s_W.svg), public domain.
- `wikidata.svg`: [Wikidata logo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wikidata-logo.svg) by Planemad, public domain. Original SVG, unchanged.
- `wikimedia-commons.svg`: [Wikimedia Commons logo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Commons-logo.svg), Wikimedia Foundation, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Original SVG, unchanged.
- `openstreetmap.png`: [OpenStreetMap logo](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/File:Public-images-osm_logo.svg) by Ken Vermette, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Official wiki's 120-pixel PNG rendering, unchanged.

Data attribution links to [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

## City icons

- `cities/rome.svg` is an original Mira illustration of the Colosseum. It uses open linework to show the curved facade and the break in the upper wall.
