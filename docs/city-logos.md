# City logos

Use the approved Rome Colosseum in `public/icons/cities/rome.svg` and its blue variant as the visual reference when creating or changing a city logo.

## Drawing style

- Draw a recognizable city landmark as an abstract architectural line drawing in SVG, with transparent interiors and background.
- Preserve credible proportions, perspective, and characteristic details. Keep enough architectural detail to feel refined at small sizes; abstraction should come from selective detail rather than a crude silhouette.
- Use a single solid stroke color, rounded caps and joins, and a stronger outer contour. Match Rome's apparent stroke weight at the intended display size.
- Keep clear space between openings, cornices, and contours. Simplify locally wherever thicker strokes collide instead of thinning the whole drawing.
- At a narrow edge, use smaller openings or draw only the portion that fits. Keep these details solid; avoid fading strokes, gradients, and an internal divider that closes off the remaining space.

## Color and placement

| Surface | City logo color |
| --- | --- |
| Blue city header | Brand yellow (`#ffda1a`) |
| Yellow homepage CTA | Atlas blue (`#345568`), matching the CTA text and arrow |

Keep geometry consistent between color variants. Use the saturated brand colors rather than lighter approximations.

## Verification

Inspect the icon at its actual placement size on the intended surface: Rome currently uses a 36 × 24 px CTA icon and a 48–64 px wide header icon. Check that openings remain distinct, strokes do not merge, and edge details sit within the outer contour. Also inspect an enlarged rendering to catch stray segments and overlaps.
